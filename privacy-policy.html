When you submit a lead form or message us through an ad, we may receive:

  • Your name

Sharing

Your details are shared only with the business that ran the ad you responded to, and only so that business can follow up on your inquiry.

How long we keep it

We keep lead information only as long as needed to handle your inquiry, and delete it on request.
